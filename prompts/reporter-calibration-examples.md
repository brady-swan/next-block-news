# Reporter calibration — learn the distinction, not the headline

Revision: **owner-calibration-2026-09-14-v2**.

Historical teaching cases supplied by Brady, NOT today's publication assignments or verified
news facts. September 14 packet: 11 YES, 25 NO, 2 CONSIDER after his clarifications; September 10
adds 12 positive references. A YES endorses editorial fit at the time, not every factual claim,
phrase or visual. Reasons below are our synthesis unless explicitly attributed to Brady.
Do not target this approval percentage or add every example's author to a guide roster.

## 1. Observed monetary conditions, not market reassurance

**YES:** [Kobeissi: ten-year yield crosses 5%, mortgage rates above 7%](https://x.com/KobeissiLetter/status/2099504260886999509).
Also [Bitcoin News' short version](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom/status/2099499799418438112).
The milestone, meaningful comparison and borrowing-cost connection do the work. They are
two examples of ONE event, not two drafts. The inspected multi-year/intraday charts make the
threshold visible; the format need not be rejected just because it uses candles.

**NO:** [Five-year Bitcoin power-law projection](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom/status/2099460904010260811),
[November as a market turning point](https://x.com/BitcoinArchive/status/2098476353787961693),
or [Bitcoin "likes" Washington news](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom/status/2099335892028522995).
An observed comparison is useful; a promised destination or invented market emotion is not.

## 2. A coherent picture can be the story

**YES:** [Bitcoin, PPI, oil, yields and Fed expectations](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom/status/2098403875073950045);
[weekly ETF outflows](https://x.com/BitcoinArchive/status/2098730789957480931).
Also earlier [US–China yield divergence](https://x.com/KobeissiLetter/status/2098108744051655003)
and [oil/inflation/Fed explanation](https://x.com/scottmelker/status/2098233002073391467).
These connect important conditions or present an intelligible aggregate. The inspected ETF
chart uses a clear zero line and weekly comparison. Credit the measurement source, not merely
the account that reposted its chart. Do not add a tour of every fund or turn context into a trade.

**NO:** [A familiar personality says handouts raise asset prices](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom/status/2099475640210657607).
The topic overlaps, but a familiar opinion alone provides less than a concrete observation.

## 3. Real movement in a developing story can precede final passage

**YES:** [Confirmed CLARITY vote schedule](https://x.com/BitcoinArchive/status/2099484453617745970),
[a route to advancing debate](https://x.com/BitcoinArchive/status/2099501976534810683),
[18-state AG action](https://x.com/BitcoinArchive/status/2099510030697426961),
and [the substantive ethics/text agreement](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom/status/2099326869535379838).
The owner accepts consequential process milestones and substantive changes. One approved post
is long; that does not waive readability, and a short-only template would miss the lesson.

**NO:** [An inconclusive adviser meeting](https://x.com/BitcoinArchive/status/2099114972986941699),
[routine caucus scheduling](https://x.com/BitcoinArchive/status/2099230005171458193),
or [one senator reiterating opposition](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom/status/2099290568102703590).
Do not cover every utterance. Ask whether this changes what readers understand or should watch.

## 4. Rescue a worthwhile angle from a weak opening

**CONSIDER, revised by Brady from NO:**
[Developer protections / criminal liability](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom/status/2099466111909494940)
and [proposed transaction-freezing powers](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom/status/2099179339006804309).
His explicit direction: both could be considered; lead with the actual meat, not scheduling.

For a fresh, supported version, start with what the provision would change for developers or
people's ability to transact. Put the calendar after it. Verify the operative version and
attribute interpretation; do not present a disputed claim about a proposal as an enacted power.
CONSIDER is not unconditional approval. This does not reverse the separate YES for a genuine
new consequential vote time. Selection and information order are distinct decisions.

## 5. Privacy fits; each incident must still earn attention

**YES:** [Argentina's international tax-data sharing](https://x.com/BitcoinArchive/status/2099508955441500303).
**NO:** [This Revolut customer-data leak](https://x.com/BitcoinArchive/status/2098766813043532053).
Brady explicitly clarified the latter: the event's significance was too small; the topic fits.
Do not learn a ban on privacy/KYC, foreign policy or data breaches, or invent an incident-size
threshold. Scale, practical consequence and what is new inform the call.

## 6. A Bitcoin company name is not the significance

**NO:** [Strive's routine purchase](https://x.com/BitcoinArchive/status/2099478727415439841),
[a predicted Strive purchase](https://x.com/BitcoinArchive/status/2098488888834531570),

## 7. Useful curiosity and education, not filler

**YES:** [Experimental HashFly mining concept](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom/status/2099498348516405303).
**NO:** [Pizza-heater miner](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom/status/2099122583023067615)
and [routine one-block reorg](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom/status/2099490489376391522).
Our tentative reading: room for intriguing science/Bitcoin ideas, less for routine mechanics
or a cute gadget. Brady did not specify a universal distinction; don't turn this into a rule
that only grand breakthroughs qualify. The HashFly original describes scaling conditionally;
the demo/video was NOT verified in our review. Interesting does not mean technically proven.

Earlier **YES:** [Fold's educational comparison](https://x.com/fold_app/status/2098103233348788246).
**NO:** [A familiar Bitcoin-revolution slogan](https://x.com/BitcoinArchive/status/2098470399881232756).
Teach something or reveal a useful perspective rather than repeat an affirmation.

## 8. Media should carry information

Brady specifically liked [a clip to post and comment on](https://x.com/RpsAgainstTrump/status/2098116568433484211).
The interest is consequential monetary-policy claims made in an interview, not permission to
adopt the reposting account's partisan framing. The original clip must be inspected before
quoting. Our calibration pass did NOT verify its audio or every image in either packet.

Selected visuals actually inspected: the yield milestone charts, ETF-flow bars, and a
highlighted Senate schedule excerpt. These reduce reading effort. A chart need not imply a
forecast; a source excerpt can be the right visual. An illustration is not factual evidence.
Create an original evidence-backed chart when useful; don't wait for decorative media.

## Generalize to today's material

Notice what the reader gains: a consequential change, useful comparison, clear explanation
or worthwhile curiosity. Identify the strongest angle rather than inheriting the guide's
opening. Keep attribution, dates and qualifications honest. Express it in simple sentences
and short, blank-line-separated paragraphs. These contrasts should sharpen your judgment,
not make you hunt for an exact matching example or produce one post from every category.
